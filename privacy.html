Privacy Policy — Cheats & Guide for GTA 6

Effective date: 2026-11-08

Unofficial fan app. Not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of its own. Your language choice, cached content and a launch counter are stored only on your device.

Personal data

The app does not collect personal data.

Notifications

The release-day reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Network

The app downloads a public content file (content.json) from our server. Standard server logs (IP address, time) may be kept for up to 30 days for security and are not used to identify you.

Advertising (version 1.1 and later)

The app may show ads from Bigo Ads (BIGO Technology) and Mintegral. Ads are switched on or off by a public configuration file (ads.json) on our server. When ads are on, the ad SDKs receive from your device: the advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in Android settings), device and OS model, language, network type, IP address and approximate location derived from it, app interaction data (ad impressions and clicks) and crash/diagnostic data. They use it to show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud. See their policies: Bigo Ads privacy policy, Mintegral privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
